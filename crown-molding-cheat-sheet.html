📏 Identify Your Spring Angle
How to find it in 30 seconds
Check the label — look for "52/38" or "45/45" on the packaging or end of the molding. The second number is your spring angle.
Method 1
Lay flat & measure — place molding back-side up on a flat surface. Measure the angle between the back flat and the table surface. That's your spring angle.
Method 2
When in doubt: most crown molding at Home Depot and Lowe's is 38° spring angle. Try 38° first.

❌ 3 Most Common Mistakes
📐
Assuming 90° — Most corners measure 88°–92°. Even 1° off creates a visible gap once painted. Always measure with a digital angle finder before cutting.
📏
Wrong spring angle — Using 38° settings on 45° molding produces cuts that look almost right but fit terribly. Check your molding label before anything else (look for "52/38" or "45/45").
🔄
Outside corner orientation — For outside corners, the ceiling (top) edge goes against the fence — opposite of inside corners. This trips up even experienced DIYers.
✅ Get Angles for Any Corner
